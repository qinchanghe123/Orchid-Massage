About Us

A calm place to relax

Orchid Massage offers a clean, private, and peaceful environment designed for comfort and renewal.

Relaxed surroundings, quiet care

Our massage space is created for guests who want to slow down, release daily stress, and enjoy a peaceful break from a busy schedule.

With a clean setting, private rooms, and a calm atmosphere, every visit is focused on helping you feel comfortable, restored, and renewed.

Professional Pain Relief Focus

Focused care for body pain and tension

Orchid Massage provides professional massage care from training-school background, focused on relaxation and common discomfort areas. Sessions can help ease shoulder pain, back tension, lower-back soreness, and everyday muscle fatigue.

Shoulder Pain Focused massage care for tight shoulders, neck tension, and upper-body stiffness.
Back Pain Relaxing bodywork designed to help reduce back tension and improve comfort.
Lower-Back Soreness Targeted care for lower-back discomfort caused by stress, long sitting, or daily strain.
Muscle Fatigue A soothing session to help tired muscles feel lighter, calmer, and refreshed.

FAQs

Before Your Visit

How early should I arrive?

Please arrive about 5–10 minutes early so you have time to check in and relax before your session begins.

Can I get a massage if I have an injury?

Please let us know about any injury before your session. For recent, serious, or painful injuries, check with a medical professional before booking.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please call as early as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule, so the appointment time can be made available.

Is there parking available?

Please call ahead if you need parking details for your visit.

Ready to Book?

Use online booking or call Orchid Massage to schedule your session. Hot Stone is free, and Cupping is available for $20.
